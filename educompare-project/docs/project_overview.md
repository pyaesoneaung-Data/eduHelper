# Project Overview

This document describes the purpose, goals, and audience for the EduCompare project.

1. Project title
 - UniMatch

2. Problem Statement
 - Lately, many high school graduates (especially First-generation international students—those without a family history of overseas education and comes from middle class family) aspire to study abroad. However, they are distracted by social media like TikTok instead of focusing on their academics. This creates a perfect opportunity for dishonest education agents. They promote low-tier universities as top-rated and budget-friendly. Once there, students relaise that while tution might be cheap, the cost of living is huge. They end up trapped and unable to return, leaving parents worried and victimized by these education scams. It is a heartbreaking situation for parents.

3. Project Summary: The International Student Transparency Portal (Thailand & Taiwan)
    - The Mission
    To empower first-generation, middle-class international students with a data-driven alternative to commission-based education agents. This platform replaces "marketing hype" with verified facts, allowing students to navigate university selection in Thailand and Taiwan safely and independently.

    - The Problem
    Students without family experience in studying abroad are frequently misled by predatory agents and deceptive social media advertising. These "middle-men" often provide false information regarding tuition costs, university quality, and legal work rights, leading to financial hardship and academic mismatch for vulnerable families.

    - The Solution
    A web-based data portal that aggregates and visualizes official university data to provide an "unfiltered" view of higher education. Key features include:

    - Verified Cost Analysis: A breakdown of total costs (tuition + hidden fees) to counter "cheap fee" scams.

    - Curriculum Profiling: A data-backed classification of degrees as "Practical/Vocational" vs. "Theoretical/Research" to ensure career alignment.

    - Regulatory Guardrails: Real-time data on visa requirements and legal part-time work hours for international students in Thailand and Taiwan.

    - Direct Comparison Engine: A tool for students to compare multiple universities side-by-side based on data metrics rather than agent recommendations.

    - The Data Approach
    As a data analyst-focused project, this app utilizes Python to clean and process datasets from official Ministry of Education sources and university registries. By centralizing fragmented information into a single, user-friendly dashboard, the project restores transparency to the international enrollment process.
