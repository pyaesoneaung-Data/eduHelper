### Flash page slogan

**“Cheers to the first — far from home, close to a bigger purpose.”**

This line is for students who are the first in their family to study abroad.

Being the first means more than just going to another country.  
It means stepping into the unknown without a clear path, carrying expectations, sacrifices, and hopes that are bigger than your own.

“Far from home” reflects the distance — physically and emotionally.  
“Close to a bigger purpose” reflects the reason — building a better future not only for yourself, but for those who depend on you.

This platform is built for those who go first.
